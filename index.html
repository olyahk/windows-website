About me

From Ukraine to Iowa

I moved from Ukraine to the United States five years ago, starting my journey as an exchange student at West Marshall High School. Now, I’m studying computer science and psychology at the University of Northern Iowa and working as a software engineer at John Deere.

My studies connect two sides of my work: how software works and how people use it. Alongside engineering, my experience includes data analysis, community development projects, and starting and managing my own business.

I’m inspired to build websites that make people’s lives easier and give business owners peace of mind. I want to simplify everyday management and scheduling, help businesses serve more customers, and free up time for the people behind them. Branding and aesthetics are part of that work too: a website should feel easy to use, look thoughtfully designed, and reflect the business it represents.
